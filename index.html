Why Medicine

To me, working in healthcare means combining a passion for education, empathy, and collaboration to make a direct impact on people's lives. I feel most fulfilled when serving a greater purpose than myself. I am deeply drawn to the lifelong learning that defines medicine and the flexibility it offers to care, teach, lead, and conduct research. My humanities education at Claremont McKenna College has further shaped my desire to approach medicine with curiosity, compassion, and a broader understanding of the human experience.
